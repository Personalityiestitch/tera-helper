## 🧩 About

**TERA Helper** is a comprehensive overlay for TERA featuring DPS meter, aggro tracker, cooldown timers, loot logger, and minimap markers.

Based on popular tools like **TERA Toolbox**, **Shinra Meter**, and **Tera DPS**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Combat Analysis
- DPS Meter — real-time damage per second for you and your party
- Aggro Tracker — see who holds aggro and threat changes
- Damage Breakdown — view skill-by-skill damage contribution
- Healing Meter — track healing done and received

### ⏱️ Timers & Alerts
- Cooldown Timers — track skill and potion cooldowns with visual bars
- Buff/Debuff Tracker — monitor active effects on you and your target
- Enrage Timer — countdown for boss enrage phases
- Mechanic Alerts — audio and visual warnings for key mechanics

### 🗺️ Utility
- Loot Logger — record all drops with timestamps and item links
- Minimap Markers — add custom markers for NPCs, nodes, and chests
- Auto-Repair — automatically repair gear at vendors
- Chat Filter — filter out gold spam and unwanted messages
